What the notes observed on 9 October

Access unchanged, as observed 9 Oct 2026, 05:16–05:17 UTC

  • Signups had been open since 27 Sep, 22:33 UTC, and new accounts got no free credit.
  • The API was operational, with no status-page event since 29 Sep.
  • Price ($0.042 per million input tokens), limits (100K tokens per second, 80 requests per second), model (jev-1.13.0), aliases (jev-latest and jev-preview both pointed to jev-1.13.0) and all six route IDs were unchanged.

Subprocessors: unconfirmed

The notes record 13 subprocessors on TypeSafe's trust center on 9 Oct, up from 6 on 7 Oct, with no update post. They name 7 new entries: Microsoft Azure, Cloudflare, Google Cloud Platform, Stytch, WorkOS, Loops and ClickHouse. The source of this observation was not preserved. The site's checks on 10 and 11 Oct read 6 entries. Unconfirmed. The data-privacy page retains its stated evidence date.

  • Official sites and resellers: all 5 sites returned HTTP 200 at the 9 Oct, 05:17 UTC recheck.
  • OpenRouter route metadata: the notes record a 64,000-token context and 57,600 maximum completion tokens on OpenRouter's Jev endpoint. They say this is route metadata, not a change to TypeSafe's limits. No other page on the site states the 57,600 figure.
  • Two version checks, failure paths unchanged: oh-my-claudecode checked at v5.6.2 (454bae0): failure path unchanged. In its opt-in active mode, Jev now also decides detector points and can end a persistent-mode loop. jev-mcp checked at 0.14.1: failure path unchanged; @jkudish/jev-agent-tools 0.2.0 does not change it.
  • Patch releases noted, not re-audited: @effect/ai-typesafe 4.0.2; LiteLLM 1.104.1 and 1.104.2; ai 7.0.136; @ai-sdk/typesafe-ai 3.0.17; @openclaw/typesafe 2026.9.9. The notes judged that none needed a failure-path diff.

Reranking and RAG proposed in the notes, not adopted

Current page: Jev reranker and Jev for RAG

"Of 5 Jev rerankers and context filters read in source, 3 still put text Jev never judged into the prompt when Jev errors (Spring AI, LlamaIndex, LiteLLM); 2 raise an error instead."

Proposed key finding; labelled Documented, 9 Oct 2026

Studies, as the notes framed them: iwhalen (grade A) measures agreement with human relevance grades and system ranking, not reranked retrieval. The reranking-pipeline studies by jev-reranker (grade B) and LanceDB (grade B) come from parties that ship a Jev reranker. zhuyansen/jev-search-rerank-eval (c896f14, 164 queries) comes from a party that ships none. It reports +0.012 nDCG@10 [−0.013, +0.037] when Jev reranks bge-m3's top 30 Reported. The notes read only that study's README, not its evaluation code.

Open questions the notes left unresolved

The cited evidence does not resolve the questions below. Later dated reports on the same topic do not by themselves validate these notes.

  1. When Jev errors, does the Hermes jev-approvals plugin stop and ask a person, or can a fallback model approve the command? The notes read Hermes core call_llm at v2026.9.24 (f97608f, agent/auxiliary_client.py lines 7610–7634 and 7749). It first retries, then falls back to other configured LLM providers on connection, timeout, exhausted-429, quota and malformed-response errors, even for an explicitly chosen provider, before it re-raises. A fallback model could answer APPROVE, and core runs an APPROVE without a person. The notes did not read whether the plugin's provider errors take that path, so they left the cell as not recorded. If the cell stayed fail-closed, their error row would read 39 / 8 / 3 / 14 / 5 / 0 and the key finding "39 stop or hold". Later reports: the 8 Oct report records a fallback to the main agent model; the 11 Oct report adds "with no main model configured, it asks you". The site does not say whether the plugin's own provider errors take the path the notes describe.
  2. With only two counted rows, where should pg-redact and docjev sit in the failure tally? The notes proposed Content moderation and Frameworks. Later report: the 8 Oct report placed pg-redact under Content moderation and docjev under Email and lead.
  3. With no Jev key, should openlayer jevals count as fail-closed or as falling back to another configured backend? With no backend and no credential it raises BackendError (backends/__init__.py lines 52–55); with another credential set, it uses that backend, which can be an OpenAI or OpenRouter LLM judge. Counted as fallback, the notes' no-key row would read fail-closed 34, fallback 10. Later report: the 8 Oct report counts it as a fallback to another backend, and fail-closed when no other backend is configured.
  4. What do jev-curator's malformed, below-threshold and no-key cells say? The notes read fail-closed, held and fail-closed, which would make "3 of 40 not recorded" on the coding-agents page 0 of 40. Later report: the 8 Oct report filled the same three cells.
  5. Which rh-guard line supports the low-confidence cell? The notes point to Jev confidence below 0.5 in score.ts (lines 110–116) and say the 0.45 in kinds.ts (line 36) is a different measure, a risk-score review cut; the cell itself is unchanged. Later report: the 8 Oct report clarifies the same distinction. The site links score.ts lines 98–127, not the notes' 110–116.
  6. Benchmarks ledger hygiene. The notes asked to lift LanceDB's "provisional" flag at grade B, label B16's operator as "alexmolas.com (individual blog)", replace creators' personal names in the leads paragraph, add a B29 note, and add zhuyansen/jev-search-rerank-eval as a new reranking study (grade A, provisional). Later report: the 8 Oct report covers the first four. No dated report adds the zhuyansen study.
  7. Is the use-cases hub line for Elastic B14 ("grade A provisional") stale? The notes say it has been grade B since 7 Oct. Later report: the 8 Oct report says it was fixed.
  8. Should pg-redact be described as "storing text unredacted" or "showing a span unmasked"? The notes say it stores the raw body and masks at display time, and that the 7 Oct report line ("that text is not redacted") stays true. No dated report on the site takes this up. Current pages say "stores … unredacted".
