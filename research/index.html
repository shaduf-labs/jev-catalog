Jev (TypeSafe AI) catalogue research history and evidence method

Every claim on this site comes from a dated research run. This page lists all reports, newest first, explains the evidence labels, and records what changed in each release, including where every earlier URL now points.

Short answer

36 dated reports cover 20 September to 24 October 2026, the newest being the research of 24 October (jev-latest on TypeSafe's API moved from jev-1.13.0 to jev-1.14.0 between the 23 and 24 Oct checks, with no dated announcement, while Opper's typesafe/jev-latest still gave 1.13; four decision leaderboards that list Jev are described on the benchmarks page with vendor rank claims checked against their data; four new graded studies bring the ledger to 45 rows; OpenAI's Decisions API now prices cached input). Before that, the research of 23 October (of 8 SDKs and integrations read in package files, 5 send images to Jev and 2 of those add TypeSafe's opt-in header for you; pydantic-ai refuses images and both LangChain packages pass them as text; TypeSafe's n8n node 0.10.0 adds an Images field with its failure behaviour unchanged; jev-latest still pointed to jev-1.13.0 on 23 Oct; all six routes list a Jev 1.14 ID; OpenAI's Decisions API became generally available on 22 Oct). Earlier, the research of 21 October (coding agents counted 14 integrations that act on Jev's answer out of 27 read in source, after ohmyjev's pi and omp adapters were added; on a Jev error the 14 are 3 fail-closed, 9 fallback and 2 fail-open; auto-mode and jcode were read but are not settled; ohmyjev's row on the When Jev fails map names pi and omp and the map still counts 80; Nace.AI's website terms and its Console terms differ on training and retention, both quoted on the alternatives page). No update was published on 9 October; separate evidence notes from 9 October on Jev rerankers and SQL and document projects are not counted among the reports. Each is kept unchanged at its original address. Facts on the question pages carry the date of the report that checked them; a later page edit does not re-date an old fact.
