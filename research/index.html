Jev (TypeSafe AI) catalogue research history and evidence method

Every claim on this site comes from a dated research run. This page lists all reports, newest first, explains the evidence labels, and records what changed in each release, including where every earlier URL now points.

Short answer

24 dated reports cover 20 September to 8 October 2026, the newest being the research of 8 October (two new use-case pages, on Jev as a reranker for RAG and on Jev inside SQL and document pipelines; the failure checklist recounted at 69 implementations; one coding-agent cell corrected). Each is kept unchanged at its original address. Facts on the question pages carry the date of the report that checked them; a later page edit does not re-date an old fact.
