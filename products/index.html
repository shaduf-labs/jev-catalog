Products and projects using Jev

This is an evidence inventory, not a customer list. A means inspected call/source path, B means a first-party repository describes a Jev integration but its exact call file was not inspected in this run, and C means a scripted or mocked example. A or B does not establish current operation, users or production deployment.

Bounded finding: none of the rows audited through 26 September 2026 independently verified a currently working production Jev product or customer deployment. This does not mean none exists.
