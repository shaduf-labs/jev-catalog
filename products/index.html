Products and projects built on Jev

The public ecosystem is moving quickly, but “built on Jev” covers demos, integrations, and directories as well as products. This page keeps those evidence levels separate.

Current finding: no verified production product was found in the sources checked on 20 September 2026. The projects below have direct Jev evidence, but none has public evidence here of customers, production deployment, or a commercial operating service.

What would upgrade an entry?

A verified product entry needs a direct product, organisation, repository, deployment, or case-study source that explicitly shows Jev use—such as a Jev import, endpoint/model reference, or first-party implementation claim—plus evidence that it is more than a local demo. GitHub stars, a directory listing, or a screenshot alone are not enough.

Demo

Shows the idea

Useful for learning; execution may be mocked or fixture-backed.

Integration

Calls or wraps Jev

Good direct evidence of wiring; still not evidence of customers.

Verified product

Runs in the world

Requires a stronger source: deployment, company statement, case study, or reproducible production trace.
