Does this task fit Jev?

Answer three questions. The check runs only in your browser; it sends nothing and does not call Jev.

1. Can you list the possible answers in advance?

For example named queues, labels, yes/no, or an ordered rubric of up to 10 levels. Arithmetic and date comparisons belong in code, not in the question.

2. Will your own code decide what happens after the answer?

Permissions, thresholds, side effects and irreversible actions stay in code.

3. What happens when Jev is unsure, wrong or unavailable?

A rule, a larger model or a person handles low-confidence answers and errors. For a yes/no question, the middle probabilities are the unsure band.
