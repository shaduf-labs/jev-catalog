Can I get a Jev (TypeSafe AI) API key today? Signup, waitlist and free-credit timeline

In its first two weeks Jev went from early access through a waitlist, to open to everyone, to signups paused, to signups reopened without free credit for new users. Some third-party pages still show an old state (four are listed below). This page gives the current status with its source and time, and every earlier change with its primary source.

Short answer

Yes. Anyone can sign up at console.typesafe.ai again since 27 September 2026, but new accounts get no free credit for now, so you pay the list price ($0.042 per million input tokens) from the first request. If you do not want a TypeSafe account, OpenRouter serves Jev with its own key and states that no waitlist or separate TypeSafe account is needed. The pool could not observe the signup page itself: console.typesafe.ai still returned HTTP 403 to its client on 5 Oct, so the status rests on TypeSafe's CEO statement, its status page and its docs. A Hacker News user wrote on 28 Sep (13:33 UTC) that they "made typesafe.ai account and put down $5"; the comment does not say whether any credit was offered. No second first-hand report has appeared since. Reported

What to do now

  1. Direct: sign up at console.typesafe.ai and create a key. Expect no free credit as a new user until TypeSafe says otherwise.
  2. Through a gateway: OpenRouter needs only an OpenRouter key ("There's no waitlist or separate TypeSafe account"). Vercel AI Gateway, Cloudflare Workers AI, Netlify and Opper each need their own account. Compare them on Where to use Jev.
  3. Do not buy from lookalike sites. Several sites resell Jev at 3–16× the list price. Check any site against official vs reseller first.
