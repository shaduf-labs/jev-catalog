Can I get a Jev (TypeSafe AI) API key today? Signup, waitlist and free-credit timeline

In its first two weeks Jev went from early access through a waitlist, to open to everyone, to signups paused, to signups reopened without free credit for new users. Many third-party pages still show an old state. This page gives the current status with its source and time, and every earlier change with its primary source.

Signups are open. New users currently get no free credit ("temporarily", per TypeSafe's CEO, 27 Sep 2026, 22:33 UTC). No later statement restoring the credit was found when checked on 28 Sep 2026, 19:50 UTC. Documented
Short answer

Yes. Anyone can sign up at console.typesafe.ai again since 27 September 2026, but new accounts get no free credit for now, so you pay the list price ($0.042 per million input tokens) from the first request. If you do not want a TypeSafe account, OpenRouter serves Jev with its own key and states that no waitlist or separate TypeSafe account is needed. The pool could not observe the signup page itself: console.typesafe.ai returned HTTP 403 to its client.

What to do now

  1. Direct: sign up at console.typesafe.ai and create a key. Expect no free credit as a new user until TypeSafe says otherwise.
  2. Through a gateway: OpenRouter needs only an OpenRouter key ("There's no waitlist or separate TypeSafe account"). Vercel AI Gateway, Cloudflare Workers AI, Netlify and Opper each need their own account. Compare them on Where to use Jev.
  3. Do not buy from lookalike sites. Several sites resell Jev at 3–16× the list price. Check any site against official vs reseller first.

Timeline of access changes

  1. 15 Sep 2026Early access; developers admitted from a waitlist

    "Today, we are opening early access and bringing developers off the waitlist as quickly as we can." TypeSafe launch post. Documented

  2. 20 Sep 2026, 21:30 UTCOpen to everyone, no waitlist

    "Jev is now available to everyone. No waitlist." @typesafeai on X. Outlets dating this 21 Sep are in later time zones. Documented

  3. 20 Sep 2026$5 new-user credit (about 120M tokens): reported, with a conflict

    No primary TypeSafe source for the amount was found. AI FrontPage (22 Sep) and CryptoBriefing (21 Sep) report "$5 in credit (120 million tokens)". lmspedia reports that its own 22 Sep signup "found no credit balance anywhere in the console". The CEO's 27 Sep post implies that new-user credits existed before 27 Sep, without an amount. Reported

  4. 20–21 Sep 2026API and console incident, then recovery

    "We are back!" (@typesafeai, 21 Sep, 09:53 UTC). The status page shows 20 Sep "Down for 18 minutes" and 21 Sep "API issues, Down for 2 minutes". Documented

  5. 22 Sep 2026, 06:19 UTCSignups paused; existing accounts keep working

    "we have to temporarily pause signups for Jev. We need to ensure quality of service for our existing signups, which will continue to function." @typesafeai on X. Documented

  6. 23–24 Sep 2026Two API latency incidents

    Resolved 23 Sep, 20:56 UTC and 24 Sep, 07:18 UTC (status page). A third-party page reported signups still paused on 24 Sep. Documented

  7. 27 Sep 2026, 22:33 UTCSignups reopened; free credit disabled for new users

    "good news: ANYONE CAN SIGN UP … bad news: we had to temporarily disable the free credits (just for new users)". Diogo Almeida on X, TypeSafe's CEO per typesafe.ai/team. No matching @typesafeai post was found. Documented

  8. 28 Sep 2026"Degradation in API Traffic", resolved 16:07 UTC

    "Elevated latency and error rates are resolved." Status bar: "Down for 11 minutes". Documented

Post times are decoded from the X post IDs (offline arithmetic) and match the dates shown in the embeds. X posts were read through X's public embed endpoint without logging in. All sources were read on 28 Sep 2026, 19:41–19:50 UTC.
