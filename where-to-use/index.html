Data and operational checks

  • Pin jev-1.13.0 if thresholds depend on a particular model. Recheck aliases and limits before a pilot.
  • Use synthetic or redacted state until the selected account's access, retention, credits and terms are accepted. A public listing is not a successful Jev call.
  • TypeSafe's legal index distinguishes no customer-data training from retention and separate enterprise zero-data-retention. Review the MCA, privacy policy and DPA for the actual workload. The MCA displayed a 23 Sep update in the 24 Sep check; its exact diff was unknown.
  • Handle documented 401, 422, 429 and 529 responses and provider outages with a code-controlled fallback.
Context note: TypeSafe direct lists a 64k whole-request budget and 32k state-plus-longest-question limit. Gateway cards showing 32k do not establish the same fan-out budget for those routes. Test the selected account rather than copying a limit across providers.

All mutable route claims above were checked against linked public provider sources on 25 September 2026. No account login, charge or live request was verified. Second source pass.
