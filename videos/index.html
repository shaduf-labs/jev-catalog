Inclusion rule and what was left out

A video is listed only if the creator's chapters or description show Jev being run on a concrete task (in the Playground, an SDK, curl, n8n or an app) and a chapter marks where that starts. The link points to the start of that chapter. Videos with a TypeSafe participant are listed but flagged, because they are not independent.

Left outVideos
Overview, news, review or interview10
Talks about Jev without showing it working4
Compilation of other people's demos2
Shows an alternative model only (Laya or Kev), not Jev2
Mock responses, no Jev call (creator's own disclosure)1
Excluded in total19
Held: a demo is claimed, but the start could not be verified from chapters or description, or the author or date could not be established13

78 distinct videos were inspected on 28 September 2026 (76 on YouTube, 1 on X, 1 on Loom). Excluded and held videos are not linked. Channels that re-upload or farm other people's content, named in the 28 September audience research, stayed excluded and were not re-inspected. The two Laya and Kev comparison videos are alternatives material, not Jev demos; see Alternatives.
