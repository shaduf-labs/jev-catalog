Alternatives · checked 25 September 2026

Open-source alternatives to Jev

Choose the layer you need to replace: independent model weights, a compatible-looking API, a local runtime, a classifier, or a generative workflow. Similar request shapes do not establish equal accuracy, calibration, speed or security.

Current finding: TypeSafe Jev weights were not publicly located or published in the sources checked 25 September. Independent open-weight Jev-like models now have directly inspectable repositories and model cards. They are not TypeSafe Jev weights, and no same-workload parity test was run. The older six-row audit was a bounded 22 September snapshot, not a current claim that no open weights exist.
