Jev: use cases, alternatives, products, and access

A source-led catalogue for builders comparing TypeSafe AI's System One model with rules, classifiers, general LLMs, and local experiments.

Jev is not a chat model. It takes state plus typed questions and returns a choice, a score, or a yes/no probability that your program can branch on.

Short answer: Jev is intended for bounded, repeated judgments such as routing, scoring, ranking, and guardrails. Keep thresholds, permissions, side effects, explanations, and human fallback in ordinary code.
Access refresh: the current TypeSafe model page names jev-1.13.0, with jev-latest and jev-preview currently pointing to it. Direct pricing is $0.042/M input tokens with free output, but console access, credits, and eligibility are unverified. A waitlist link was observed on 20 Sep but not in the fetched home text on 24 Sep; that does not prove open signup. Compare every route.
24 Sep change-detection: the independent proxy pricing page displays tiered prepaid rates, and TypeSafe's MCA displays a 23 Sep update date; the exact legal diff is unknown. No account access or sensitive-data route was verified. See the bounded report.

Builder decision · synthesis 24 Sep 2026

BranchUse it whenAction
PilotThe task is a bounded Choice, Score, or Noul decision; the selected route has a key and credits; data terms are accepted; and a fallback exists.Use direct TypeSafe first, pin jev-1.13.0, and log errors and fallback outcomes.
Route fallbackDirect TypeSafe access is blocked and OpenRouter's account, logging, billing, and terms are acceptable.Treat OpenRouter as a Jev access route, not as a different open model.
SubstituteThe blocker is the interface, local scoring/classification, or generation layer.Use the named adapter, local project, classifier, or structured-output LLM for that layer. None is verified Jev-model parity.
WaitEligibility, credits, price, quota, terms, or data controls are unknown.Use synthetic or redacted state and recheck the selected route.
StopThe task needs open-ended writing or code, a deterministic policy, an automatic irreversible action, or has no acceptable fallback.Keep the rule, human review, or another approved path.

The 24 September synthesis is a decision guide, not an access, adoption, or performance measurement. The bounded product audit checked on 23 September 2026 found no verified production product in its sources. Read the synthesis report.
