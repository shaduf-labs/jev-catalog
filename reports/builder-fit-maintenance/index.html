Builder fit and maintenance synthesis

This report turns the accepted access, alternatives, and product records into a builder decision branch and proposed page-maintenance rules.

Scope: No new product, alternative, candidate, benchmark, or independent measurement was added. The synthesis uses the 20 Sep access record, the 22 Sep alternatives audit, and the 23 Sep product audit.

Route fallbackDirect TypeSafe access is blocked and OpenRouter'"'"'s account, logging, billing, and terms are acceptable.Use OpenRouter as a Jev access route. It is not a different open Jev model. Check the selected account and bill.
