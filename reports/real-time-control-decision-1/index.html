4. New page: real-time control demos

The page is Jev plays Doom: what real-time demos do when a Jev call fails. Its title keeps "(TypeSafe AI)". "jev plays doom" was the first suggestion for the prefix "jev plays" on Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo at 12 Oct 2026, 05:16 UTC (R14-S26). The full table, the patterns to copy and the list of what was not verified are on that page; this section records the counting decisions.

Counting rule, 12 Oct 2026:

  • A demo is counted when it calls TypeSafe's Jev by default and its own code acts on the answer (counting criterion). JevPlaysSnakeGame, doom-jev and jev-robotics-demo are counted (decision 1).
  • llm-invaders is listed as a research harness and not counted: no model player is the default, and it reaches Jev through OpenRouter.
  • ra2web is listed as a design contrast: it "calls a local substitute by default; not counted".
  • "Keep the last action" counts as "action goes ahead".

Reason: when a call fails, the loop keeps acting on a choice Jev did not make for this tick. The action that goes ahead is the previous choice repeated, not a new unchecked action, and the pages say so next to the count.

Effect: 3 counted rows meet the 3-row rule for a domain table, so When Jev fails gets a new domain, "Real-time control (games and simulations)".
