Research reportChecks 19 October 2026, 05:22–05:41 UTC

Failure map completed with ohmyjev, jev-mcp commits, and three hosted decision models (19 Oct 2026)

This dated report records the evidence behind the update of 19 October 2026. It adds ohmyjev to the When Jev fails map, states which commit the jkudish/jev-mcp cells were read at, aligns the failure terms on two use-case pages with the map, and reads the published prices and data terms of three hosted decision models from other providers for the alternatives page. Nothing was run: no account, key, API call or third-party code.

Key findings

When Jev fails: For all 80 counted implementations, every failure cell (no key, error, malformed answer, below threshold and bypass) is recorded from source at pinned commits (19 Oct 2026).

Documentedohmyjev added (79 → 80); the published totals were first reproduced by script from the map's cells, then recounted, 19 Oct 2026

Of 80 Jev implementations read in source, on a Jev error 40 stop or hold the action, 22 fall back, 10 let it through, 5 return no decision and 3 are advisory. The 10 that let it through are 4 moderation bots, 4 agent hooks, a passage filter and an eval tool.

Documentedsource at pinned commits, 19 Oct 2026 (40 + 22 + 10 + 5 + 3 = 80; 4 + 4 + 1 + 1 = 10)

jkudish/jev-mcp: every published failure cell was read at 0.11.0 (commit 53fe575); 0.13.0 (5e0ca5c) and 0.14.1 (86eae78) are later checks in which the failure path was unchanged.

Documenteddated read notes from 29 Sep, 2 Oct, 8 Oct and 14 Oct 2026, one source file re-read 19 Oct 2026; the earlier difference between pages was a labelling difference, not a cell disagreement

Alternatives: Of 37 listed substitutes: 13 model weights, 11 local runtimes, 4 request adapters, 2 classifier/LLM approaches, 7 hosted APIs from other providers (OpenAI, Microsoft, Cloudflare, Subquadratic, Liquid AI, Mouse, Upright).

Documentedcounts checked by script over the table rows, 19 Oct 2026 (13 + 11 + 4 + 2 + 7 = 37)

Access: unchanged at 19 Oct 2026, 05:22 UTC. jev-latest still points to jev-1.13.0 and jev-preview to jev-1.14.0.

DocumentedTypeSafe models page and status page, 19 Oct 2026, 05:22 UTC (R19-S04, R19-S05)

Checks: 19 Oct 2026, 05:22–05:41 UTC (access 05:22–05:24; scan, look-back and drift 05:25–05:28; ohmyjev and jev-mcp 05:22–05:31; hosted decision models 05:22–05:30; small items 05:34–05:39).

The research notes and the source ledger (R19-S… IDs) are held in the pool's private record. Key sources are linked below and on the pages each finding feeds. Checks on the sources: 125 cited IDs checked, 0 missing; 17 sentences that split a total were checked by script so that their parts add up and every category with 3 or more items (or 10% of the total) is named.

Rules kept: nothing was installed or run, so no Jev or other provider call was made, and the pool has no account with any of them. Failure cells come only from source at pinned commits. Vendor quality claims are Reported. Nothing in this report is Tested.

1. The failure map now counts 80: ohmyjev joins

ohmyjev (78bb82c) is a Claude Code module with Bash, Write/Edit and exfiltration gates that deny by default, plus adapters for Codex, pi and omp. On 18 Oct it was shown on the Claude Code page but not counted, because its pi and omp adapters had been read only at their tool-call gates. On 19 Oct both adapters were read in full at the same commit (651 and 768 lines), with the shared session code and every handler in the Claude Code module. With the 18 Oct reads, every entry-point file from the one 78-file listing has now been read for its Jev paths; the built Codex hook was checked at its gate lines and its source was read in full. The bypass cell is therefore recorded and ohmyjev is counted (R19-S200 to R19-S207).

The Claude Code page cited 86eae78 and the map cited 53fe575 for the same MCP server. The dated read notes settle it: every published cell (no key, error, malformed answer and below threshold on 29 Sep; bypass on 14 Oct) was read at 0.11.0, 53fe575. 0.13.0 (5e0ca5c, 2 Oct) and 0.14.1 (86eae78, 8 Oct) are later checks in which the failure path was unchanged. No cell changed (R19-S212 to R19-S216). The no-key error is raised inside a dependency that was not read; the published cell ("the tool call returns an error") rests on the 29 Sep read.

3. Three hosted decision models from other providers

On 18 Oct Liquid AI d1-ultra and Mouse-1 were known only from their launch pages, and Eevo's terms had not been found. On 19 Oct each provider's own docs, pricing page and legal terms were read. All three now have a row on alternatives in the layer "Hosted API from another provider": your requests go to that provider, not to TypeSafe. No graded comparison with Jev exists for any of them; each vendor's claims against Jev are its own (Reported), and each sells a competing model.

  • jev-latest → jev-1.13.0, jev-preview → jev-1.14.0; the models page is byte-identical to 16 and 18 Oct, and no date for retiring 1.13 is published (R19-S05).

6. Smaller findings

  • Vercel's own adoption figures. A DEV.to post claimed "35% of paid-for groups in Vercel". Its source is Vercel's own AI Gateway production index: Jev reached "35% of paid teams in its first two weeks" (35.3% in its table) but "accounted for just 0.27% of gateway spend over that same period"; 55.6% of week-one teams returned in week two. These figures cover paid AI Gateway teams only; Vercel sells gateway access to Jev; the post's publication date was not read on the page. Added to products beside the VentureBeat-relayed Vercel claim. Reported (R19-S64)
  • VentureBeat. The two relayed claims were not rechecked again (HTTP 429; no archived copy found); their line keeps its 18 Oct date (R19-S60, R19-S61).
  • Version notes. litellm 1.106.0 (18 Oct; minor; failure path not rechecked at this version), @openclaw/typesafe 2026.10.5 and oh-my-claudecode v5.7.4 and v5.7.5 (patches; not re-read) (R19-S50). n8n's missing-credential path is byte-identical at n8n@2.44.3 and 2.44.1 (R19-S56).

7. Evidence gaps

  • ohmyjev: the built Codex hook was checked at its gate lines, not line by line (its source was read in full). Its pi and omp decision handlers outside the tool gate are not rows.
  • jkudish/jev-mcp: the no-key error is raised in a dependency that was not read.
  • Hosted models: none was called, so the vendors' request shapes and their compatibility with TypeSafe's SDKs are untested. Liquid's errors and rate limits were not found; Cloudflare was not checked for d1-ultra; Eevo's region was not found and its legal pages carry no date.
  • Vercel's production-index post: publication date not read on the page; two spend figures for different periods are quoted, not reconciled.
  • Leads seen in the scan and not read: an open-weight 31B model, Basal-Omni, whose publisher calls it "nr 1 on JevBench"; Cribl decision 1.0 weights on Hugging Face; a DEV.to comparison naming "Drex" (not identified).
  • Reddit returned HTTP 403 and X has no search access; YouTube, TikTok, GitHub trending and GitHub code search were not covered. Those surfaces are unknown, not empty.

  • Nothing was run: no account, key, API call, package install or third-party code.
  • Scan window: this update's scan covered 18 Oct 05:16 UTC to 19 Oct 05:21 UTC. It starts at the previous scan's cutoff, so there is no gap and no overlap. The 72-hour decision-API look-back starts at 16 Oct 2026, 05:21 UTC and overlaps the previous look-back, as intended; it found no new hosted decision-model launch beyond those already recorded.
