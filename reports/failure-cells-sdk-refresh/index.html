Jev (TypeSafe AI) research report, 14 October 2026: what implementations do with no key, code paths that skip Jev, and the Python and TypeScript SDKs

This dated report records the evidence behind the update of 14 October 2026. It settles the remaining no-key cells on When Jev fails and most of the open bypass cells, refreshes the Python and TypeScript SDK pages, adds two graded comparisons to benchmarks (B43, B44), and adds SubQ Snap as a row on Jev alternatives. Nothing was run: no account, key, API call or third-party code.

Key findings

When Jev fails: With no API key, 50 of 77 implementations refuse to start, error or hold; 17 fall back, 7 proceed as if allowed and 3 are advisory (source read at pinned commits, 14 Oct 2026).

Documentedfrom source at the pins; some cells are judgements; recounted by script, 14 Oct 2026

When Jev fails: Code paths that never ask Jev are recorded for 66 of 77 implementations; 11 are not recorded (judge tools 4, frameworks 3, browser agents 3, email 1) (source read at pinned commits, 14 Oct 2026).

Documenteda bypass cell counts as recorded only when every entry point was read

Python SDK: Install typesafe-sdk 0.7.4 (released 9 Oct 2026). Defaults: 10 s timeout, 2 retries on 408/429/5xx within 30 s; a missing key raises TypeSafeError when the client is created (Documented, package read 14 Oct 2026).

TypeScript: TypeScript today: @typesafe-ai/sdk 0.6.0 throws at client creation without a key; the AI SDK provider 3.0.18 throws LoadAPIKeyError at the first call; ai 7.0.140 renamed experimental_evaluate to experimental_decide (Documented, 14 Oct 2026).

Documentedpublished package files read 14 Oct 2026, 16:47–16:53 UTC; nothing installed or run

Benchmarks: 41 graded rows (A 12, B 14, C 8, D 7), with B43 (SubQ Snap vs Jev, grade B) and B44 (a four-model Tetris round robin, grade A).

Reportedresults in the operators' own unitsDocumentedmetric and results files opened at pinned commits, 14 Oct 2026

No page sums or ranks the graded comparisons into an overall verdict: each result belongs to its task.

Checks: 14 Oct 2026, 16:46–16:53 UTC; strict bypass recount, 14 Oct 2026, from 17:12 UTC.

The research notes and the source ledger (R16-S… IDs) are held in the pool's private record. Key sources are linked below and on the pages each finding feeds. Checks on the sources: 151 cited IDs checked, 0 missing; every sentence that splits a total was checked by script so that its parts add up and every category with 3 or more items (or 10% of the total) is named.

Rules kept: nothing was installed or run, so no Jev, Subquadratic or other provider call was made, and the pool has no account with any of them. Failure cells come only from source at pinned commits; n8n's own behaviour comes from its source at release n8n@2.44.1. Package facts come from the published package files, opened without installing. Study results are the operators' own measurements. Nothing in this report is Tested.

Previous report: 13 Oct research report. All dated reports: Research.

  • Jev implementations at pinned commits: 31 open cells on build/when-jev-fails (no key, bypass and n8n below-threshold) for ticket triage, email and lead sorting, n8n nodes, browser and computer use, and three single rows (jev-trader, hermes-jev-mana, jev-mcp). Each cell rests on a file and line range at the published pin (R16-S100 to R16-S134, R16-S300 to R16-S321).
  • n8n at release n8n@2.44.1: the host's missing-credential path (node-execution-context.ts L409-432, R16-S122 to R16-S124; tag list R16-S125).
  • SDK packages: typesafe-sdk 0.7.4 (wheel), @typesafe-ai/sdk 0.6.0, ai 7.0.140 and @ai-sdk/typesafe-ai 3.0.18 (tarballs and single files), read without installing (R16-S200 to R16-S216; spot-check R16-S68).
  • SubQ Snap: Subquadratic's docs, Terms and launch post (R16-S217 to R16-S227).
  • Two comparison repositories at pinned commits: kprzybys/snap-vs-jev-swe at b754c44 (R16-S66) and dante01yoon/decision-model-arena at 285c803 (R16-S67). All 11 comparison repositories on the lead list were pinned from their commit feeds (R16-S65).
  • Standing checks: access and notices, the comparison card's price and status cells, the discovery scan with the 72-hour decision-API look-back, and the drift list.

2. Access status and home notices

Checked 14 Oct 2026, 16:46 UTC: Documented. Unchanged since 27 Sep 2026, 22:33:25 UTC.

  • Signups open with conditions; new accounts get no free credit. The console and sign-up page returned HTTP 403 (a Cloudflare challenge), so sign-up was not observed directly (R16-S01, R16-S02).
  • Status page: "All services are online", "Last updated on Oct 14, 2026 at 4:46pm UTC" (R16-S04). It now also lists two regional API entries, api.us-east-1.typesafe.ai and api.us-east-2.typesafe.ai; TypeSafe's docs still name only api.typesafe.ai. No access change.
  • Price ($0.042 per million input tokens; output free), limits (100K tokens per second, 80 requests per second), the model jev-1.13.0, its aliases and the model IDs on all six routes are unchanged (R16-S05, R16-S11, R16-S30, R16-S34 to R16-S36).
  • Shown on Home (2): new-user credit disabled; lookalike resellers. Hidden (1): Series A (retires 16 Oct). Retired (1): TypeSafe's Jack & Jill case study, at the end of its seven-day shelf life (checked 14 Oct 2026, 16:47 UTC); it was never shown on Home. Its card no longer appears on TypeSafe's blog index; the post itself is still online (R16-S08, R16-S18). It stays a vendor lead on benchmarks. New: 0.
  • Jev was not listed in Microsoft Foundry's catalog or Amazon Bedrock's model cards when checked on 14 Oct 2026, 16:48 UTC; Microsoft-Decision-1 was still listed as Preview (version 1) in Foundry (R16-S37 to R16-S42).
  • Subprocessors were not rechecked (the trust portal needs a browser render); the 11 Oct value stands.

  • OpenRouter's status value −2 has no documented meaning on the endpoint page.

  • SubQ Snap: numeric rate limits were not found in its docs or Terms at 14 Oct 2026, 16:48 UTC; whether Jev's SDKs work against it by changing the base URL was not tested.
