Legal and data distinctions

TypeSafe’s MCA still displayed last updated 23 September; the earlier 20 September record displayed 19 September, and the 24 September catalogue had already noted the newer date. The exact clause diff is unknown. Current text permits service, fee, telemetry, fraud and legal processing and says model-weight training on Customer Data needs prior consent. The privacy policy still displayed 19 November 2025; no training or fine-tuning on Input does not establish a Jev-specific retention duration. The DPA still displayed 24 April 2026. Enterprise ZDR is separate from ordinary public access. Gateway badges and options should not be combined into an end-to-end promise without account and application checks.

Newly discovered, not proved new in the window

The GitHub Jev topic showed QuantDinger “Updated Sep 26,” with no hour or content diff. Its creator README describes an optional TypeSafe Jev pre-trade Choice entry gate, configured with key, base URL, model and timeout. Exits bypass it; provider errors fail open, and absence of Jev may lead to LLM fallback or no-AI pass-through. This is a documented integration, not an observed Jev call, verified trading result or safe automated financial control. The repository’s broader size or public URL does not prove use. The community Jev CLI/Agent Skill likewise describes direct TypeSafe or OpenRouter setup and version 0.3.2 without an exact in-window release time; it is not official TypeSafe.

An espetro Kev-compatible fork release was dated 23 September 08:16 UTC, outside the window and not TypeSafe Jev weights. Laya metadata was last modified 24 September 05:39:22 UTC; its card points to the declared implementation, but separate he-jev/laya file identity remains unverified. JevAny code and its Qwen base do not establish inaccessible adapter-file hashes or licences.
