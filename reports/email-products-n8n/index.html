S3. use-cases/email-and-lead-sorting: meets the ship condition

  • Five projects read at pinned commits: Jevmail f6f20af (unchanged since the 28 Sep audit), the ilyamk Gmail labeler ade42bf, the parth-kp IMAP classifier db96194, vynnlee jev-mail 61e346f, and spam-leadgen-pipeline 78a7592 (lead qualification). Selection: GitHub stars in a search with 59 results, plus one lead from S1. A reading order, not a survey.
  • Counts (Documented; 5 projects chosen by stars, a reading order, not a sample): 3 of 5 can write to the mailbox; 0 of 5 have delete or trash code, although vynnlee requests the full https://mail.google.com/ scope and parth-kp has full IMAP access; 0 of 5 change the mailbox on a Jev error. For unsure mail, 1 of the 3 writers labels it "Review" (vynnlee), 1 applies the label anyway (ilyamk), and 1 marks it processed and never re-checks it (parth-kp). 4 of 5 cut the body before sending (1,000 to 5,000 characters); parth-kp sends it uncut.
  • Cost per 1,000 emails (Documented price; pool arithmetic assuming 4 characters per token plus 500 tokens of question text, both assumptions): about $0.004 for very short emails; about $0.04–$0.15 at the four projects' body cuts; up to about $1.34 if every email filled Jev's 32k state. A demo figure of "1,700 emails for $0.18" (Reported, 18 Sep) works out to $0.106 per 1,000.
  • Title query source: Google suggested "jev email classifier" for "jev email" (05:22 UTC).

S4. oh-my-claudecode v5.6.0: version check done

  • Pinned: tag v5.6.0, commit e74b22c. The compare from v5.5.0 shows 165 commits touching 25 Jev paths.
  • Unchanged (Documented): on a timeout, HTTP error or invalid response the heuristic twin decides and the resolver "never throws"; a point stops calling Jev after 3 consecutive failures; nothing is sent unless OMC_JEV lists points; excerpts are cut to 200 characters; points still run in shadow by default.
  • Changed (Documented): default timeout 2,000 ms (was 250); new activation OMC_JEV=<point>:active or all:active, which makes Jev decide and prints a warning; a new script-side judgment channel with the same degrade-never-block rule; the request shape is fixed.
