What changed for Jev builders, 8 to 9 November 2026

Three published Jev benchmark studies were graded from their own files, so the benchmarks ledger now has 48 graded rows. Two leaderboards were re-read: one is unchanged apart from a longer list, the other changed its index. auto-mode 4.0.0, a Claude Code permission mod that asks Jev, was read in source: its failure path is unchanged and it stays unsettled. Nace.AI's legal pages now agree with each other; OpenCode's two texts still do not. TypeSafe's aliases, model IDs, price, signup and credit state did not change.

Key findings

Three more Jev studies are graded from their files: B49 (grade B), B50 (grade A, small sample) and B51 (grade B, the author's own harness). The ledger has 48 graded rows: A 15, B 16, C 10, D 7.

auto-mode 4.0.0 handles a Jev failure as before. Its bypass now lists 7 skip stages, including a reply-channel allow added in 3.1.x. It stays unsettled; the coding-agent counts (15 + 10 + 4 = 29) and the failure map (80) are unchanged.

Nace.AI's website and Console terms, dated 6 Nov 2026, now agree: no training on your content unless you opt in. OpenCode's docs and Terms (effective 8 Nov 2026) are still not reconciled.

Is Jev deterministic?

Not fully, in one published option-order test: reordering the same three options changed Jev's route on 41–42 of 500 cases (the same route in all six orders 91.6–91.8% of the time), against 179 for OpenAI's Decisions API and 12–14 for Microsoft-Decision-1 (B41, grade A; the operator's own runs, README unchanged on 9 Nov 2026). Reported This is one task, synthetic bank-withdrawal routing, and it does not measure repeated identical requests.

TypeSafe's own figures. TypeSafe's cookbook on verifying LLM answers against their source reports, for jev-1.14.0 on 1,000 MuSiQue questions answered by gpt-6-luna: Jev's check passed 73% of the answers, 95.7% of them right, and sent 27% to review, including 84 of the 115 wrong answers. Reported TypeSafe sells Jev; its figures, outside the graded ledger and not checked independently (cookbook read 9 Nov 2026, unchanged since 8 Nov; R29-S211). Now also on Jev as a judge.

auto-mode 4.0.0: failure path unchanged, still unsettled

auto-mode is a community mod that asks Jev whether a Claude Code tool call may run without asking the user. Version 4.0.0 (published 8 Nov 2026) is a major release; the six files the coding-agents row cites are byte-identical to 3.1.6, and the 4.0.0 changes sit in a review step that runs only after a deny. The row was last read at 3.0.0 (21 Oct 2026).

Re-read on 9 November and unchanged

  • Access: signups open, no free credit for new accounts, API operational ("All services are online"; the status page's history lists no event after 30 Oct). The console showed the same sign-in page; signup itself was not observed. Home still shows two notices (no new-user credit; lookalike resellers).
  • Models and price: jev-latest and jev-preview give jev-1.14.0; jev-1.13.0 is listed with no deprecation date; $0.042 per million input tokens, output free. TypeSafe's models, images, quickstart, changelog and legal pages and its docs index are identical to the 8 Nov copies.
  • The six routes: TypeSafe, OpenRouter, Vercel, Opper, Netlify and Cloudflare list the same Jev IDs; Opper's typesafe/jev-latest still gives jev-1.13.0.
  • OpenCode's free ID (third-party route; not run by TypeSafe) is still listed as "Free" for a limited time. Lookalike sites: all three still answer, and Eye Security's report still names them.
  • The option-order figures behind the determinism answer (B41) and TypeSafe's cookbook figures are unchanged.

Not checked or not read

  • Amazon Bedrock's model lists (their static text has no TypeSafe or Jev string, but part of the list is rendered by script, so Bedrock was not determined; the 31 Oct reading stands). Foundry and Workers AI were not re-read.
  • Artificial Analysis's methodology; the position the Jev Decision Index app shows; the other tasks of S1CogBench.
  • auto-mode's mod host and its skip stages' internals; OpenCode's Privacy Policy (where "Connected Service" is defined); nace-sdk 0.7.0, @openclaw/typesafe 2026.11.2 and n8n 2.47.3.
  • Newer benchmark repositories and coding-agent projects seen in the scan were not opened. Reddit, X, YouTube and TikTok could not be searched for the hours between the checks. No request was sent to any route or provider, no package was installed or run, and no account was created.
